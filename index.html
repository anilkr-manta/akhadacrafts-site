Teak & Acacia

Full Equipment Catalog

The Complete Lineup

A reference catalog of implements from the akhada tradition — presented for education, comparison, and appreciation of the training forms.

Clubs

01 Indian Club
Indian Club Pair
Teardrop / swing clubs
Classic teardrop-shaped wooden clubs for joint mobility, shoulder health, and flow training. The foundation of Indian club practice.
02 Mugdar
Heavy Mugdar
Mudgar / meel
The heavy club of the akhara tradition. Builds rotational power, grip strength, and shoulder resilience. Hand-turned from dense kiln-dried teak.
03 Jori Clubs
Jori Clubs (Pair)
Heavy club pair
Longer and more conical than standard clubs — the extra mass generates more torque. Competed at Varanasi's Nag Panchami festival.
04 Persian Club
Parshiyan Club (Pair)
Persian / zurkhaneh club
The Persian counterpart to Indian clubs — heavier, wider base, used in the zurkhaneh tradition. Builds shoulder and wrist strength through circular swings.
05 Yoga Karlai
Yoga Karlai
Yogic club / swing implement
A yogic training club combining breath, movement, and rotational flow. Used in traditional yoga-based conditioning systems for full-body awareness.

Maces & Barbells

06 Hanuman Gada
Hanuman Gada — 2ft / 2.5ft
Standard gada mace
A heavy ball on a wooden handle. Sacred to Hanuman. Swung in large circular motions targeting shoulders, core, and grip. The icon of Indian strength.
07 Hanuman Gada
Hanuman Gada — 3ft
Competition gada
The competition-grade gada. Longer handle increases leverage and demands greater core stability. Gold gadas of this size are awarded as trophies in Varanasi.
08 Hanuman Gada
Hanuman Gada — 4ft
Heavy long gada
The most demanding gada — full-body engagement at every swing. For advanced practitioners who have mastered the mechanics of shorter lengths.
09 Bhim Gada
Bhim Gada
Warrior mace
Named after the mighty Bhim of the Mahabharata. A thick, powerful mace for raw strength development. Heavier head, shorter handle — pure power training.
10 Bhim Gada
Big Bhim Gada — 4ft
Heavy long warrior mace
The ultimate Bhim Gada — 4ft handle with a massive head. For the serious practitioner. Full-body rotational power at competition level.
11 Sagwan Gada
Sagwan Carving Hanuman Gada
Teak carved premium gada
Hand-carved from premium Sagwan (teak) with intricate detailing. A collector-grade training implement — as beautiful as it is functional.
12 Sagwan Gada
Sagwan 3-Handle Hanuman Gada
Triple grip teak gada
A unique 3-grip handle design built from Sagwan (teak). The multiple grip positions allow varied training angles and progressive loading patterns.
13 Wooden Mace
Wooden Mace
Steel mace alternative
A wooden training mace for offset-load conditioning. The uneven weight distribution challenges stability and builds functional rotational strength.
14 Macebell
Macebell
Long-handle macebell
A long-handle wooden macebell bridging traditional gada training and modern macebell fitness. Excellent for 360s, 10-to-2s, and shoulder conditioning.
15 Samtola
Samtola
Sum-tola / Indian barbell
A log-shaped wooden barbell originally used to level wrestling pit floors. Develops grip, core, and explosive rotational power. Crafted from acacia or teak.
16 Thoppi
Thoppi
Helmet weight / head implement
A traditional Indian weighted helmet implement used in akhara conditioning. Builds neck, trap, and shoulder strength through carry and movement patterns.

Stone Implements

17 Nal
Nal
Stone lifting cylinder
A hollow stone cylinder with a handle inside. A foundational akhara strength tool. One of four competition categories at Varanasi's Nag Panchami festival.
18 Gar Nal
Gar Nal
Stone neck ring
A circular stone ring worn around the neck during dands and baithaks. Builds neck, shoulder, and core strength. Gaining traction with MMA fighters worldwide.
19 Dumbbell Pair
Wooden Dumbbell (Pair)
Traditional wooden dumbbells
Handcrafted wooden dumbbells from the akhara tradition. Used in dand and baithak conditioning. A natural-grip alternative to steel, easier on the joints.

Training Accessories

20 Shena Board
Shena Board — 75cm
Standard push-up handles
Traditional wooden parallettes for the Dand — the Hindu push-up. Elevates wrists for deeper, joint-neutral range of motion. Standard 75cm size.
21 Shena Board
Big Shena Board (Sheesham / Sagwan)
Premium push-up board
The premium large shena board — available in Sheesham (rosewood) or Sagwan (teak). Wider platform, deeper range, built to last a lifetime.
22 Mallakhamb
Mallakhamb Accessories
Pole, rope, grip chalk
Equipment for India's ancient pole-and-rope gymnastics sport. Teak poles, cotton ropes, magnesium chalk, and crash mats. Various configurations available.
23 Storage
Storage & Display Racks
Club and mugdar racks
Wall-mount and floor-standing racks built for clubs, mugdars, gadas, and nals. Keeps your full akhara kit organized and displayed with respect.
